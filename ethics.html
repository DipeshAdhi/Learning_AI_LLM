Ethical use of AI

Getting it right matters more than getting it fast.

Nothing here is a legal document and none of it is about fear. It is the short list of habits that keep AI use honest — the things that protect the people whose data you paste in, the readers who trust what you publish, and your own name on the work.

Choose your situation

The rules are not the same for everyone.

Drafting an email and submitting a dissertation carry very different obligations. Pick the one that matches what you are actually doing.

If you remember one thing

You are accountable for anything you send, submit, or ship. “The AI wrote it” has never once worked as a defence — not for a bug in production, not for a false claim in an essay, and not for a citation that turned out not to exist.
